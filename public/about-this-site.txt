The Examined Classroom is a personal education site for classroom-ready thought experiments, AI ethics guidance, and practical educator resources. It is written for K-12 teachers, school leaders, students, and families.

Teacher resource pages include standalone classroom-ready pages for the Paperclip Maximizer, The Consciousness Line, AI authorship, AI detector false positives, biased classroom robot scenarios, school surveillance, AI grading mistakes, and AI policy design staff activities.
